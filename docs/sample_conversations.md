# Sample Conversations

## 1. Black holes
User: Why does a black hole bend light?
Assistant: Because gravity curves spacetime, and light follows the curved geometry.

## 2. Pluto
User: Why was Pluto reclassified?
Assistant: Because it did not clear its orbital neighborhood.

## 3. Dark matter
User: What is dark matter?
Assistant: It is matter inferred from gravitational effects, not direct light emission.

## 4. Cosmic perspective
User: Why do scientists care about cosmology?
Assistant: Because it tells us where the universe came from and how structure formed.

## 5. User memory

Assistant: I will keep that in mind for later explanations.

## 6. Teaching style

## 7. Multi-turn recall
User: Compare this to what you said earlier.
Assistant: I will refer back to the previous explanation and keep the same framing.

## 9. Clarification
User: Does this mean the universe is infinite?
Assistant: Not necessarily. Observational limits and geometry matter.
